Mammoth {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installing into namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

The cluster starts in safe mode and is read-only until workers have reported
their blocks. Watch it leave:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec deploy/{{ .Release.Name }}-gateway -- \
    mammoth admin safemode status

Then open the UI:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-gateway 8080:8080
